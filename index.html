Our story

A tiny corner for cozy coffee moments.

Sips Coffee Corner began with a simple idea: coffee should feel warm, playful, and part of your daily rhythm.

We created our shop for people who want more than just a drink — they want a small pause. From the first sip to the last, every cup is made with care, softness, and a little bit of Berlin spirit.
