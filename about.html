About us

Every sip
feels soft.

A tiny Berlin corner for warm cups, slow starts, and the kind of coffee moments you actually remember.

The beginning

A small pause in the middle of the city.

Sips Coffee Corner began with a simple idea: coffee should feel warm, playful, and part of your daily rhythm.

We built Sips for people who want more than a quick drink. Students settle into the window seats. Remote workers find the quiet table. Friends stay a little longer than planned. The coffee is serious, but the feeling is soft.

From the first sip to the last, we want every cup to bring comfort, care, and a little bit of Berlin spirit.

What we believe

Warm, careful,
never rushed.

01

Coffee should feel human.

We keep the menu focused, the service kind, and the atmosphere easy to settle into.

02

Quality can be gentle.

Good beans, careful milk texture, and consistent recipes — without the gatekeeping.

03

The neighborhood comes first.

We source locally where we can and try to be the kind of place people feel proud to have nearby.
